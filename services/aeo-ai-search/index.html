What this service involves

Answer Engine Optimisation (AEO) focuses on making information clear, accurate and accessible for systems that provide direct answers. AI search visibility builds on sound website fundamentals: useful content, clear entities, crawlable pages and trustworthy information.

Understand the customer need

Start with the questions customers actually ask. Research the language they use when comparing options, understanding costs, checking processes or evaluating providers. Organise content around those needs and answer each question directly before adding supporting detail.

Build a clear foundation

Clarity matters. Define specialist terms, include the context needed to avoid misleading statements and support claims with appropriate evidence. A short answer is not automatically a good answer if it leaves out important qualifications.

Create useful, specific information

Technical accessibility remains important. Ensure key pages can be crawled, use descriptive page titles and headings, maintain sensible internal links and avoid hiding essential information behind inaccessible interfaces. Structured data can help describe eligible entities and content, but it does not guarantee inclusion in AI answers.

Connect the experience

Consistent business information helps establish who a business is, what it offers and where it operates. Keep the website, business profile and reputable external references accurate. Use clear organisation, service and location details where appropriate.

Review and improve

Measure AI-search performance carefully. Track referral visits where available, branded and non-branded discovery, qualified enquiries, customer-reported discovery sources and changes in the questions that lead people to the business. Reporting tools may not expose every AI-generated impression or citation.

Understand the customer need

AEO is not a shortcut around SEO. Strong technical foundations, helpful content, clear business information and real expertise support both traditional search and newer answer-led discovery. Review changes over time and prioritise improvements that help people first.
