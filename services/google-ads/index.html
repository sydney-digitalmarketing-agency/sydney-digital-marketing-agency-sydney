What this service involves

Google Ads can place relevant offers in front of people searching for a product or service. Effective campaigns depend on clear objectives, focused targeting, useful landing pages and disciplined measurement.

Understand the customer need

Campaign planning should begin with the business outcome. Decide whether the priority is qualified enquiries, calls, purchases or another measurable action. Build the campaign structure around themes that reflect customer intent, rather than grouping unrelated searches together.

Build a clear foundation

Keyword selection and match settings influence which searches can trigger ads. Search-term reviews help identify irrelevant traffic, new opportunities and negative keywords. Ad copy should accurately reflect the offer and set realistic expectations before someone clicks.

Create useful, specific information

The landing page is part of the campaign, not an afterthought. It should continue the message from the ad, explain the service clearly, load reliably on mobile and make the next step easy. A mismatch between the search, ad and page can waste budget and reduce trust.

Connect the experience

Conversion tracking is essential. Configure meaningful actions, test that they record correctly and distinguish valuable leads from low-quality or duplicate activity. Use the data to understand cost per lead alongside lead quality and eventual business outcomes.

Review and improve

Budgets and bids should be reviewed in context. Avoid making large changes based on tiny samples or short-term fluctuations. Test one meaningful variable at a time where possible and document what was changed.

Understand the customer need

Paid search works best as a managed learning process: plan, launch, monitor search terms and conversions, improve landing pages and refine the account as evidence accumulates.
