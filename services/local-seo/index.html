What this service involves

Local SEO improves how a business appears when people search for services in a particular area. It brings together accurate business information, location relevance, useful local pages, reviews and a consistent online presence.

Understand the customer need

Start with the business profile. The name, address, phone number, opening hours, categories and services should be accurate and consistent. Choose categories that genuinely describe the business, add clear service information and keep important details current.

Build a clear foundation

Your website should reinforce the same identity. A well-structured contact page, clear service descriptions, location information and useful local content help visitors understand where you operate and whether you serve their area. Create location pages only when they provide genuinely distinct value.

Create useful, specific information

Reviews can help people evaluate a business. Encourage genuine customers to share honest feedback, respond professionally and use recurring comments to improve service. Never buy reviews or pressure customers to leave only positive feedback.

Connect the experience

Local citations and directory listings can support consistency when they are accurate, relevant and maintained. Prioritise reputable platforms rather than submitting the same details to large numbers of low-quality directories.

Review and improve

Track profile interactions, calls, direction requests, website visits and qualified enquiries. Compare performance over time and check whether the visibility is reaching the locations and customers that matter.

Understand the customer need

Local search is not a one-time setup. Update business details when they change, publish useful updates where appropriate and review the customer journey from discovery through to contact.
